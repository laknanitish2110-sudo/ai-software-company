## What They All Have in Common

1. **Vertical focus** — Each dominates ONE domain (legal, coding, support, sales)
2. **Real output** — Not just chat, but actual work product (code, contracts, resolved tickets)
3. **Tool use** — Agents connect to real systems (GitHub, CRM, databases, phone networks)
4. **Human-in-the-loop** — All maintain human oversight for critical decisions
5. **Enterprise-first** — Most revenue comes from enterprise contracts, not consumer
